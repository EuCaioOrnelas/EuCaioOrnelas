## ⚡ About me

I'm **Caio Ornelas**, founder and software engineer focused on building products at the intersection of **software, AI, automation and business operations**.

My main project is **Wiize**, a B2B software ecosystem designed to help companies prospect, manage, engage and operate their commercial processes through technology.

I'm interested in building software that goes beyond interfaces — from the **product experience to APIs, integrations, automation, data and infrastructure**.

> **I don't just write code. I build systems around problems.**
